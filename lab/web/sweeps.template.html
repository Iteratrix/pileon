How these numbers were made

__CORPUS_NOTE__ Replies and quotes are labeled with codebook v2 (supportive, neutral, critical, hostile, abusive) by a local model given the target post as context; "hostile" counts hostile or abusive replies aimed at the target.

Baselines are the target's 200 posts from just before the incident, matched by kind (reply or top-level) when at least 10 exist. Engagement counts are as of October 2026. Follows are dated by their record keys, so "followed beforehand" is as of the incident. Ratios add one to both sides so a zero median stays finite.

This page holds no account identifiers, handles, post text or dates. Follower counts are rounded to two significant figures.
